How to Use Facebook Desktop

This guide shows you how to interact with Facebook Desktop — from logging in and navigating the interface to creating content and engaging with posts.

1. Signing In

To start using Facebook Desktop, launch the app after installation and log in with your Facebook credentials.

*If you encounter issues logging in (e.g., being redirected to a QR code or login prompt), make sure your Facebook account has a password set and isn’t solely linked via Facebook, as this can interfere with login workflows.* (Community tip: some users solved login problems by adjusting their Facebook security settings before attempting desktop login.)

2. Navigating the Interface

Once logged in, you’ll be greeted with the main Facebook interface — typically a timeline of posts from your friends or suggestions of content. The layout is designed to resemble a familiar social feed, optimized for desktop screens.

Facebook also lets you switch between view modes to customize how content appears in your feed, similar to the official Facebook web experience.

3. Creating a Post

To post something:

  1. Click the “New Post” button in the top area of the interface.
  2. Type your message — keep it engaging and concise!
  3. You can include photos, videos, emojis, or links if supported by the client.
  4. Press “Post” to publish to your profile and your followers’ and Facebook friends feeds.

In general, on Facebook platform you create content by clicking the composer, writing your message, and adding any media you want to include.

4. Interacting with Posts

Once posts appear in your feed, you can:

  • Like a post by clicking the heart icon.
  • Comment to join conversations on a post.
  • Repost / Quote to share it with your followers and friends with your own commentary.

These interactions help you engage with your community and get more visibility. Most desktop interfaces follow similar interaction patterns to the web or mobile versions.

5. Searching Friends & Following Users

Use the search bar (typically at the top or in a navigation pane) to find users or keywords you’re interested in. Once you find someone you want to follow, hover or click their profile and tap “Add friend” to add them to your friend list.

This makes it easy to explore topics and build up a feed of content that matters to you.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Keep posts visual and engaging — adding a photo or video often increases interactions.
  • Use relevant hashtags and mentions (@username) to expand your reach.
  • Post consistently to grow your audience and maintain visibility in followers’ feeds.
